title: "Hamming, Rank, and Sum-Rank: Different Metrics for Different Equivalence Problems"
type: "Seminar (online)"
---

**Speaker:** Giuseppe D'Alconzo.

In this talk, we explore the problem of determining whether two linear codes are isometric with respect to three different metrics: the Hamming, rank and sum-rank metrics. For each metric, we define the corresponding computational problems. We discuss the connections between these problems, as well as related open questions. Moreover, we highlight their applications in cryptography.
